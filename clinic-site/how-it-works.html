How it works

Four simple steps. No app to install, it works in your phone browser.

1

Choose your area

Pick the area you are in. You see the clinics there with their current wait, shortest queue first.

2

Get your token

Enter your name and mobile number. Your queue number appears immediately.

3

Follow your place

The page refreshes every 15 seconds and shows who is being served, how many are ahead of you, and your estimated wait.

4

Go in when called

When the receptionist calls your number, the page tells you it is your turn.

Good to know

  • The wait time is an estimate based on the clinic's average time per patient. A long consultation can change it.
  • Plan to arrive when only one or two patients are ahead of you.
  • You only see how many people are waiting, never other patients' names or numbers.
  • QueueEase is not for emergencies. If it is urgent, go to the nearest emergency room.
