For clinics

Replace the crowded waiting room with a simple token system you set up yourself.

Quick setup

Register your clinic, set your average minutes per patient and a staff PIN.

One-button reception

Your receptionist presses one button to call the next patient.

Protected by PIN

Only your staff can call patients. Other clinics cannot touch your queue.

Daily reset

Token numbers start from 1 every day, separately for each clinic.

Live status for patients

Patients follow their place on their own phone, so fewer people crowd the waiting room.

Visible to nearby patients

Once activated, your clinic appears in your area list with its current wait.
