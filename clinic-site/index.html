How it works for patients

No app to download. It works in your phone browser.

1

Pick your area and clinic

See nearby clinics with their current wait, shortest queue first.

2

Get your token

Enter your name and mobile number. Your queue number appears right away.

3

Watch your turn come

The page updates by itself with who is being served and your estimated wait.

For clinics

Set up your own token system in minutes. Your receptionist calls the next patient with one button.

Your own setup

Register your clinic, set the average time per patient, and choose a staff PIN. You get a patient link and a receptionist link.

Protected by PIN

Only staff with your clinic's PIN can call the next patient. Other clinics cannot touch your queue.

Fresh queue every day

Token numbers start from 1 each day, separately for every clinic.

Found by nearby patients

Once activated, your clinic appears in your area's list. Patients only see counts and wait times, never other patients' details.

Monthly subscription. A clinic is activated after its first payment is confirmed.

Questions

Do patients need to install an app?

No. Everything works in the phone browser.

What do patients see about other patients?

Only how many are waiting and the estimated time. Names and phone numbers are never shown.

How is the estimated wait calculated?

By the number of patients ahead multiplied by the average time per patient that your clinic sets.

Will patients get WhatsApp messages?

This is coming soon. For now the live status page shows the queue.

Give your patients a shorter wait and your staff a simpler day.
